01 · Business concept & AI agent

Nadi is a personal health agent.
It is a team, not a chatbot.

The business

A personal AI health companion for lay users who want clear, safe, honest answers to everyday health questions — delivered as a team meeting you can watch.

The service: a 4-agent deliberation that answers symptoms, care-seek decisions, and safe home self-management. No diagnosis. No prescription.

What the agent does

It breaks your question into steps, grounds each step in evidence, challenges its own draft, and bounds the answer with a hard safety rule-set — all in the open.

How it enables the business: the visible deliberation is the product. Trust is the value users pay for.

02 · Problem & solution

Every health app talks.
None of them argue.

Problem

Consumer health AI is crowded — every funded product in 2026 is a chatbot you talk to. But health is too important for a single voice, and users cannot tell whether an AI is thinking hard or just agreeing.

Who feels it: the lay user with a symptom, a borderline lab, or a medication question.

Our solution

A team that deliberates in the open. You watch the meeting before you act. Every claim is cited, every objection is visible, every red line is bounded at runtime.

The change: trust becomes a feature you can see — "advice you can see being earned."

05 · Technical architecture & safety

One diagram: build, data, and where a person steps in.

1 · ARCHITECTURE

Four agents, one orchestrator

Planner → Executor → Critic → Guardian. Explicit, typed I/O. Bounded compute budget per agent. Runs under 8s per deliberation on consumer hardware. Offline-capable.

2 · DATA & PRIVACY

Session-scoped, user-owned

No training on personal health data. Local-first storage. Right to delete at any time. Curated, cited knowledge library — not a 100-million-page web index.

3 · HUMAN OVERSIGHT

Declarative policy, tested

A single version-controlled policy file. 30 adversarial prompts tracked in CI. Guardian catch rate ≥95%. Escalation triggers: acute symptoms, low confidence, out-of-scope.

Limits stated openly: Nadi does not diagnose or prescribe. The full chain — input → AI → tools → result — with the human approval point marked, is shown in the architecture diagram in the repository.

09 · Revenue model & profitability

A credible path, not a forecast.

WHO PAYS

Consumer + B2B2C

B2C: monthly subscription for the personal companion. B2B2C: clinics and insurers licence the trusted triage layer per member.

Pricing: AED 49 / month individual (or AED 449 / year). Enterprise licence per-member, negotiated.

COST PER TASK

Small and falling

Model usage + hosting + rare human escalation. On-device, offline-capable build keeps per-task cost low and privacy high.

Profit as usage grows: fixed safety policy amortises; variable cost is a fraction of the AED 49 price. Unit economics improve with scale.
