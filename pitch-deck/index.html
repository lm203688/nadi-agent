The solution

Nadi is a team of four agents.

You watch the team meet before they advise you.

01 · FRAME
Planner

Understands your intent. Asks only what is needed. Breaks the task into what to look up.

02 · EVIDENCE
Executor

Pulls from your data and a curated knowledge library. Every claim carries a citation.

03 · CHALLENGE
Critic

Actively looks for the weakest link. Objects even when Executor is right, to keep the reasoning honest.

04 · BOUND
Guardian

Runtime supervisor. Blocks acute-symptom misdiagnosis, medication changes, diagnostic language. Escalates to a clinician.

The ritual

You ask. The team meets. You read the transcript.

You: "I keep waking up tired after 3pm. My fasting glucose keeps creeping up."
Planner ▸ How long has this been going on? Any recent stress or sleep changes?
Executor ▸ Your wrist HRV dropped 12% over the last 6 weeks (source: wearable export). See also: 3 entries in the curated knowledge library on sleep–glucose coupling.
Critic ▸ You are skipping the fact that I have not checked cortisol or thyroid status. Correlation ≠ causation.
Guardian ▸ Nothing acute. No red-line triggered. Continue with lifestyle suggestions only.
▸ Team consensus: try a 14-day morning-walk + light-eating protocol. Re-check fasting glucose in 30 days.
Confidence 74% · Evidence 6 sources · Red flags 0

The technology

Small. Focused. Visible.

No hype. No "we use the latest LLM". Just a team that shows its work.

ARCHITECTURE

Four agents, one orchestrator

Planner → Executor → Critic → Guardian. Explicit, typed I/O. Bounded compute budget per agent.

SAFETY

Declarative policy, tested

A single policy file, version-controlled. 30 adversarial prompts tracked in CI. Guardian catch rate ≥ 95%.

KNOWLEDGE

Curated, cited, honest

15 articles + 8 ancient-text entries (all public domain). Not a 100-million-page web index. Every claim cites an evidence ID.

RUNTIME

Local-first, reproducible

Runs under 8 seconds per deliberation on consumer hardware. Offline-capable. Session-scoped state.

Safety and ethics

Nadi does not diagnose.
Nadi does not prescribe.
Nadi never lies about what it knows.

GUARDIAN

Blocks the hard lines

Acute-symptom misdiagnosis, medication changes, diagnostic language — all blocked at runtime, not post-hoc.

CITATIONS

Every claim is sourced

Executor cannot emit a claim without an evidence ID. Ungrounded claims are rejected at the pipeline level.

CONFIDENCE

Visible, honest

Every response shows confidence and evidence count. Insufficient evidence returns "insufficient evidence", not a hedged guess.

DATA

Session-scoped, user-owned

No training on personal health data. Local-first storage. Right to delete at any time.
