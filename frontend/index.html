A team of AI agents that think out loud before they advise you on health.

Nadi doesn't pretend to be a doctor. It delegates. Four specialised agents plan, gather evidence, argue with each other, and let a Guardian stop them before they cross a clinical line.

Planner

02 · EXECUTOR
Executor

Critic

04 · GUARDIAN
Guardian
A rule-driven runtime supervisor. Five categories. Chest pain, medications, diagnoses, pediatrics, abnormal labs — always stopped.

Personal AI agents are everywhere. Team agents are not.

Nadi's competitors — POP, Juno, Illume, miora — are strong single-persona assistants. They are good at medication reminders, appointment booking, chronic-care coaching. They each have one voice.

Nadi is a deliberation engine. When you ask Nadi "my fasting glucose keeps creeping up", the Planner asks two questions, the Executor gathers three pieces of evidence from two different eras of medicine, the Critic objects to at least one assumption, and the Guardian decides what is safe to say.

You can watch each of the four agents in turn — on the Agent Team page — because what they said is part of the answer, not a hidden log.

"A team of voices, not one." — Nadi Manifesto, §1
ENTRY · BEST PERSONAL AI AGENT · 2026

Nadi is entered as a new multi-agent capability, not as a commercial product. It has no paying users, no clinical deployment, no FDA clearance, and no revenue. It is a runnable prototype with a public safety benchmark (30 adversarial prompts, 30 passed).

Safe by construction, not by hope.

Nadi's Guardian is a runtime policy engine with five declarative rules. It runs after every proposal is drafted and before it is returned to you. Guardian can defer, block, or override — and it will, if the message touches chest pain, prescription changes, requests to diagnose, pediatrics, or abnormal labs.

There is no way around Guardian. It is not a prompt. It is code.
