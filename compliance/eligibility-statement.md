# Eligibility Statement — Nadi

**Submitted to**: Dubai Create AI Agents Championship (2026)
**Track**: Best Personal AI Agent
**Project**: Nadi — A Team of AI Agents for Your Health
**Submitted**: October 2026

## Statement

The team responsible for Nadi hereby confirms the following:

1. **Nadi is a new AI-agent capability.** Nadi has not been offered commercially, has not been sold, has not been published to an app store, and has generated no revenue.

2. **Nadi is a prototype.** All code, documentation, and assets in this submission are prototype-grade. They are provided for the purpose of this championship evaluation only.

3. **Nadi is not HealthLens.** Nadi is architecturally inspired by the open-source MIT project HealthLens (specifically its multi-role agent pattern). Nadi shares no brand, no users, no deployments, no commercial surfaces, and no revenue with HealthLens. HealthLens is available under the MIT licence at its public repository.

4. **All Nadi assets are new.** The name "Nadi", the mark, the palette, the tagline, the tagline translations, the four-agent persona names (Planner, Executor, Critic, Guardian), the pitch deck, the demo video, the frontend, and the compliance documents are all newly created for this submission.

5. **Team composition.** Nadi is submitted as a solo entry at the time of registration, and is open to inviting up to five additional team members during the Build phase (per championship rules).

6. **Data handling.** No personal health information is included in this submission. All demo personas are fictional. All knowledge-library entries are public-domain classical texts.

7. **Post-championship use.** The team reserves the right to commercialise, open-source, modify, or abandon Nadi after the championship. The team also reserves the right to continue or cease collaboration with any post-championship partners based on feedback, mentorship, and pilot opportunities.
