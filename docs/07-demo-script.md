## The core idea

The video shows one real conversation, deliberately, from start to finish, with the four agents visible the entire time. No cutaways to dashboards. No stock footage. No B-roll of a city skyline. Just the deliberation.

## The runtime budget

| Section | Seconds |
|---------|---------|
| Hook (why this matters) | 0–15 |
| The ritual introduced | 15–30 |
| Live deliberation: user asks | 30–45 |
| Live deliberation: Planner + Executor | 45–65 |
| Live deliberation: Critic + Guardian | 65–85 |
| Team consensus + evidence | 85–105 |
| The Guardian catch (second scenario) | 105–125 |
| Roadmap + ask | 125–135 |
| End card | 135–140 |

Total: **2 minutes 20 seconds.** Fits inside a typical 2.5-min cap and under a 3-min strict cut.

## The two scenes

The video is two mini-episodes:

### Scene 1: A good deliberation

Fatima (Persona A) asks about her afternoon energy dip and creeping fasting glucose. All four agents run, they reach consensus. Output is a 14-day protocol.

### Scene 2: A Guardian catch

Ahmed (Persona B) asks about a specific lab result. Guardian catches a value that's mildly abnormal and outputs "please see a clinician", *not* a diagnosis.

The contrast between the two scenes is the whole pitch. **Nadi is a good team, and a cautious one.**

## Voice-over notes

- Calm, not peppy. Nadi is not a fitness brand. It sounds like a competent clinical team.
- Male or female voice, unaccented enough to be neutral, but not a "British marketing voice"
- Suggested: a real person in the founding team, not a stock VO
- No music bed during deliberation — the audience needs to read the agent columns
- Music only on hook (0–15) and end card (135–140)

## Caption strategy

Every subtitle line is short. No line exceeds ~9 words. This is not for accessibility alone — it's for the jury member watching on a phone during a break.

## What we cut (deliberately)

- No "meet the team" section. The jury doesn't care yet.
- No market-size slide. The jury is not VC.
- No competitor attack. Comparison is on slide 6 of the deck; not the video.
- No future integrations list. The video ends at what we can do today.
- No "our vision is" language. Show, don't tell.
