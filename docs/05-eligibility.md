# 05 · Eligibility — how Nadi fits the Championship rules

## The rule that matters most

> *"Entries can be an idea, a prototype or an early-stage MVP. Existing businesses may submit a new AI-agent solution or capability. **Fully commercialised solutions are not eligible** — the goal of the championship is to grow new ventures, not to support already-established ones."*

Nadi is designed to sit comfortably inside the second half of this sentence: **an existing business's *new* AI-agent capability, not yet commercialised**.

## The posture

**Nadi is a new capability, not a repackaged product.** The three things that make this true:

1. **New brand.** Nadi is not HealthLens. The name, mark, palette, tagline, user-facing narrative are all new, created for this submission.
2. **New scope.** Nadi's scope is a single question: *"how does a team of AI agents deliberate before advising you on your health?"* HealthLens, in its own world, is a broader platform. Nadi is deliberately scoped smaller.
3. **No revenue, no users.** Nadi has not been offered, sold, shipped, or used for revenue. No users have been onboarded to a Nadi product. The prototype lives in this repository and nowhere else.

## The two sentences to say in the eligibility statement

> *"Nadi is being submitted as a new AI-agent capability developed for this championship. It is a prototype and has not been commercialised. Nadi is architecturally related to the open-source MIT project HealthLens (specifically its multi-role agent pattern) but shares no brand, no users, no deployments, and no revenue. All Nadi assets in this submission are new."*

## Common questions from judges (and our prepared answers)

**Q: Is Nadi already on the App Store / Google Play?**
A: No. Nadi is a prototype only.

**Q: Are there paying users?**
A: No. Nadi has not been offered commercially.

**Q: How is this different from your HealthLens product?**
A: HealthLens is an MIT-licensed open-source platform that includes a multi-agent subsystem. Nadi is a Championship-specific product built on the same architectural idea, with a new brand, a scoped feature set, and a new demonstration. This is analogous to how an open-source framework can inspire many distinct commercial products.

**Q: Does Nadi generate revenue today?**
A: No.

**Q: What happens to Nadi after the Championship?**
A: We will decide based on the feedback, pilot opportunities, and mentorship we receive. Nadi may or may not be commercialised; that is precisely the decision the Championship is designed to help with.
