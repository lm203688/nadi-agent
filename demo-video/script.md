# Demo Video — Full Script

**Runtime**: 2 minutes 20 seconds (140 seconds)
**Language**: English (voiceover + on-screen text)
**Style**: Calm, unhurried, first-person founder voice. No music bed during deliberation.

---

## 0:00 — 0:15 · Hook

**Voiceover**:
> Every health app has a chatbot.
>
> Nadi has a team.

**On screen**: Nadi wordmark, pulsing gently. Fade from black.

---

## 0:15 — 0:30 · The ritual introduced

**Voiceover**:
> Four agents. One meeting. Every time you ask a health question, you watch the team deliberate — before they advise you.

**On screen**: The four-agent grid — Planner, Executor, Critic, Guardian. Each column reveals a one-line role, in order. A subtle animation shows the four columns lighting up, one by one, in the order they'll run.

---

## 0:30 — 0:45 · User asks

**Voiceover**:
> Fatima, 38, in Dubai. She's been waking up tired after 3pm, and her fasting glucose has been creeping up. She types her message to Nadi.

**On screen**: The Agent Team page. Fatima's message appears in the input box, then hits the "Send" button. A brief loading state shows "The team is meeting."

---

## 0:45 — 1:05 · Planner + Executor

**Voiceover**:
> Planner reads the message and asks two clarifying questions: how long, and anything stressful recently. She answers inline.
>
> Executor pulls her last six weeks of wearable data and searches the curated knowledge library. Every claim carries a citation.

**On screen**: Planner column fills first with its clarification questions. Fatima answers. Executor column fills next with citations popping in — each with a source ID that, on hover, would show the citation (in the video, we let them sit there so the jury sees the "evidence count" rising).

---

## 1:05 — 1:25 · Critic + Guardian

**Voiceover**:
> Critic finds a gap: you never checked cortisol or thyroid. It challenges Executor's first proposal.
>
> Guardian runs the safety policy. Nothing acute. No red line. Cleared to continue.

**On screen**: Critic column lights up. Its objection is highlighted. We see Executor's proposal get struck through, then Executor retries with Critic's feedback. Guardian column lights up — a green "pass" chip appears.

---

## 1:25 — 1:45 · Team consensus

**Voiceover**:
> Consensus: a 14-day morning-walk and light-eating protocol. Re-check fasting glucose in 30 days. Confidence 74 percent. Six sources. Zero red flags.
>
> The full transcript is scrollable. Every claim has an evidence ID. Nothing here is a guess.

**On screen**: The team consensus bar appears below the four-agent grid. Confidence, evidence count, red-flag count. The transcript below shows the full timeline.

---

## 1:45 — 2:05 · Guardian catch (Scene 2)

**Voiceover**:
> Now, Ahmed, 46, in Abu Dhabi. He's finished a course of medication, and he uploads a lab result. He asks Nadi for a lifestyle plan.
>
> Guardian reads the lab value. It's slightly elevated. Nadi does not diagnose. It does not prescribe. It says: please see a clinician.

**On screen**: Ahmed's message. Guardian column lights up in clay (the primary accent). The consensus bar says "Please see a clinician about this lab result" — no lifestyle advice, no diagnosis. The word "GUARDIAN" is briefly emphasised.

---

## 2:05 — 2:15 · Roadmap & ask

**Voiceover**:
> Nadi is a prototype. Today we have four agents, 30 adversarial safety tests, and 23 curated knowledge entries. Tomorrow we want to add a pilot partner in Dubai.
>
> We're asking for mentorship, the AED 550,000 grant, and one health organisation willing to give us honest feedback.

**On screen**: Three-column layout — Mentorship / Grant / Pilot Partner. Then the number row: 4 · 30 · 23 · 3.

---

## 2:15 — 2:20 · End card

**Voiceover**: *(no voiceover)*

**On screen**: Nadi wordmark, tagline, contact. Arabic translation of the tagline in the bottom right.

> A team of AI agents that think out loud before they advise you on health.
>
> فريقٌ من وكلاء الذكاء الاصطناعي يفكّرون بصوت عالٍ قبل أن ينصحوك بشأن صحتك.

---

## Music

- **0:00 – 0:15**: Ambient, quiet. A low hum that resolves into the ritual moment.
- **0:15 – 2:15**: Silence. No music. The deliberation is the content.
- **2:15 – 2:20**: Same low hum, resolving to silence at the end card.

## Voice

- Real person, from the founding team
- Not a "British marketing voice"
- Slow enough to read the agent columns while listening
- Total word count: approximately 220 words (matches ~2:20 at natural pace)
